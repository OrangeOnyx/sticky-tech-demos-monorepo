# Raw vs wiki

Two layers, different writers:

- **Raw** — the owner pastes an article, a paper, a rant. After it lands, nobody edits the body. Provenance lives here.
- **Wiki** — the model restates what mattered, in pages typed as source / entity / concept / synthesis, linked with `[[wikilinks]]`.

If a fact only exists in raw, it is not yet in the second brain. [[raw-vs-wiki]] is the rule that keeps the archive from being confused with the knowledge base.

This playground's ingest box is the raw layer. The graph is the wiki layer. See [[llm-wiki]] and [[second-brain-os]].
