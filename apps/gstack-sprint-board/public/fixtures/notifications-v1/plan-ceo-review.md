# /plan-ceo-review — Notifications v1

**Specialist:** CEO / Founder  
**Reads:** office-hours design doc  
**Mode chosen:** HOLD SCOPE (with one selective expansion)

## 10-star product hiding inside the request

The 10-star version is not “we support five channels.” It is:

> I never wonder whether the people who should know, know.

That is a reliability product. Channels are an implementation detail.

## Four modes (scored against this request)

- **SCOPE EXPANSION** — dream the preference graph, quiet hours, digest AI, Slack threading. Fun. Wrong. This team has not sent a first message yet.
- **SELECTIVE EXPANSION** — hold inbox + email, cherry-pick one expansion.
- **HOLD SCOPE** — maximum rigor on inbox + one transactional email. **Default recommendation.**
- **SCOPE REDUCTION** — email-only. Too small; you lose the in-product habit.

## Decision

**HOLD SCOPE**, plus one opt-in expansion:

1. In-app bell with unread count and a 20-row inbox.
2. One template: “Your {object} {verb}.” Example: “Notifications v1 moved to /plan-eng-review.”
3. **Selective expansion (approved):** mark-as-read syncs across tabs. Without it the inbox feels broken on day one.

Cut: SMS, push, Slack, digest, preference center, analytics dashboard, user-authored templates.

## What would make this a 10

A founder can create a feature, advance it, and the teammate who owns QA sees one bell and one email before they are asked in standup. That is the demo. Everything else is costume.
